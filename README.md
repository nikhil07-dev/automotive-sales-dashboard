Automotive Sales Performance Dashboard

Overview

This project analyzes sales performance inspired by Tata Motors to identify growth trends, segment performance, and business opportunities using Power BI.

Objective

* Track FY23 vs FY24 sales performance
* Identify key growth drivers
* Highlight underperforming segments

Tools Used

* Power BI
* Excel
* DAX

Key Insights

* Total sales grew ~1.7% YoY
* Passenger Vehicles (PV) are main growth driver (~6%)
* Commercial Vehicles (CV) declined (~4%)
* Domestic market dominates revenue
* EV share increased significantly

Business Recommendations

* Focus on PV segment expansion
* Improve CV performance
* Expand international presence
* Invest in EV growth
